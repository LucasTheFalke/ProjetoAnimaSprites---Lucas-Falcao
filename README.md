## About the Game

Meet **Felpudo**, a surprisingly agile little firefighter with a big heart and a lot of fluff! In **Roof Rescue**, simple mechanics meet clever environmental puzzles. 

Your mission? Navigate a small downtown neighborhood under pressure. The path upward isn't straightforward—you will need to strategically push, pull, and arrange everyday objects around the alleyways to create your own makeshift steps. Once your path is paved, put your parkour skills to the test, scale the rooftops, and overcome obstacles to reach the ultimate reward: freeing **Fofura**, a sweet little trapped bird waiting for her hero!

---

## Key Features

* **Simple Mechanics:** Easy to pick up and play, focusing on fun physics-based interactions.
* **Brain Meets Agility:** Solve light puzzle challenges by positioning objects strategically before putting your parkour moves to the test.
* **Parkour Rooftops:** Jump, vault, and slide across roofs with smooth, satisfying movement.
* **Heartwarming Goal:** Climb to the highest peak to rescue Fofura the bird and bring smiles all around!

---

## How to Play

1. **Explore & Strategize:** Look around your surroundings. Identify objects you can interact with.
2. **Push & Build:** Push boxes and items into position to create stable platforms and pathways.
3. **Parkour to the Top:** Run, jump, and climb your way across the rooftops using your agile firefighter moves.
4. **The Rescue:** Reach the top cage and set Fofura free!
